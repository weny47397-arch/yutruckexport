Who We Are

Heavy-Duty Truck Exporter · Since 2018

Yu Truck Export has exported heavy-duty trucks — F / H3000 / L3000 / X3000 series — to 30+ emerging markets across Africa, Central Asia and South America since 2018. As an officially signed SHACMAN dealer (Shanxi Heavy Duty Automobile Import & Export Co., Ltd.), I source factory-direct and ship worldwide.

From single-unit orders to large fleet contracts, our team manages every step — vehicle selection, configuration, factory QC, containerisation, shipping, customs documentation, and post-delivery support.

  • Authorised signed dealer for these models (certificate No. SQIDACN47)
  • Direct factory pricing, no middlemen
  • Pre-shipment inspection on every order
  • Full export documentation (CIQ, CO, Form A/E/F)
  • Spare parts supply and technical support
  • Africa / Central Asia / South America logistics experts
