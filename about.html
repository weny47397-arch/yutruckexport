Our Story

From Single Shipments to Fleet Contracts

Eight years of SHACMAN truck exports — what we have learned along the way.

2018

First SHACMAN Shipment to Africa

Started with a single F3000 dump truck order to Zambia. Learned quickly that African buyers want clear pricing, clear documentation, and spare parts supply after delivery.

2020

Expanded to Tanzania & Nigeria

Added H3000 tractor heads to the lineup after seeing demand from cross-border logistics operators in Dar es Salaam and Lagos. Established forwarding partnerships at Mombasa and Tema ports.

2022

X3000 Introduced for Mining

First major fleet deployment: X3000 6×6 tippers to copper mines in the DRC and Zambia. Reinforced chassis, 460 hp Cummins, built for unpaved haul roads.

2024

Cameroon & Chad Corridor Launch

L3000 fuel tanker configuration developed for hydrocarbon transport. First orders shipped to Cameroon for the Chad pipeline supply chain.

2026

Online Catalogue & Direct Inquiry

Launched yutruckexport.com so fleet owners can browse configurations, see real delivery photos, and request quotes directly — no middlemen.
