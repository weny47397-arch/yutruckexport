About SHACMAN

One of China's Heavy-Duty Truck Giants

SHACMAN (Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile Co., Ltd.) is a Tier-1 Chinese heavy-truck manufacturer founded in 1968, headquartered in Xi'an — the cradle of China's heavy-duty truck industry. Operating in 100+ countries with F, H3000, L3000, X3000, and M3000 series.

Manufacturer Snapshot

Cummins · Weichai · FAST · HANDE — Tier-1 Components

Every SHACMAN truck ships with parts from globally recognised suppliers:

  • Cummins ISM / ISMe / ISD — engines from 180 to 560 horsepower
  • Weichai WP series — Chinese-made alternative, factory-tuned
  • FAST transmissions — 9 / 12 / 16-speed manual or AMT
  • HANDE axles — 7.5 to 32-ton heavy-duty drive axles
  • ZF steering — German engineering on X3000 flagship
  • JOST 5th wheel — 50 mm coupling rated 16 tons

From 6×4 dump trucks for copper mines in Zambia to 4×2 distribution vans in Lagos, SHACMAN builds for the real world — heavy loads, bad roads, and operators who can't afford downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for African Buyers

The questions our African fleet owners ask most often.

Do you have spare parts available in Africa?

Yes. We maintain spare parts inventory in Dar es Salaam (Tanzania), Lusaka (Zambia), and Lagos (Nigeria). Common wear items — filters, brake pads, belts, clutch kits, Cummins engine parts — are stocked locally and ship within 24 hours. Full parts catalogue with pricing available on request.

How long does shipping take to Mombasa / Dar es Salaam / Tema?

From Chinese ports (Shanghai / Tianjin): 20-25 days to Mombasa or Dar es Salaam, 30-35 days to Tema (Ghana) or Lagos, 35-45 days to Matadi (DRC) or Douala (Cameroon). We handle CIF / FOB and door-to-door logistics including customs clearance.

Are SHACMAN trucks suited for unpaved African roads?

Yes. Our Africa-spec configurations include reinforced double-layer chassis, heavy-duty suspension, higher ground clearance, and tropical cooling rated for 50°C. The X3000 6×6 handles 30-ton loads on unpaved haul roads (used in Zambia copper mines); H3000 and L3000 are standard for cross-border logistics.

What's the minimum order quantity and payment terms?

MOQ: 1 unit (sample orders welcome). Payment terms: 30% T/T deposit, 70% balance against B B copy. L/C accepted for orders above USD 200,000. Trade Assurance available for Alibaba orders.

Do you offer operator training and after-sales support?

Yes. Every shipment of 5+ units includes free video operator training and 12-month warranty on powertrain. For orders of 10+ units we dispatch a Chinese technician to your country for 2 weeks of on-site commissioning and mechanic training.

What documents do you provide for customs clearance?

Full export documentation: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (Form E for EAC, AGOA for eligible countries), CIQ inspection certificate, and engine emission certificate. We work with your nominated clearing agent or recommend one in each port.
