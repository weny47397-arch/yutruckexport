About SHACMAN

One of China's Heavy-Duty Truck Giants

SHACMAN (Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile Co., Ltd.) is a Tier-1 Chinese heavy-truck manufacturer founded in 1968, headquartered in Xi'an — the cradle of China's heavy-duty truck industry. Operating in 100+ countries with F, H3000, L3000, X3000, and M3000 series.

Manufacturer Snapshot

Cummins · Weichai · FAST · HANDE — Tier-1 Components

Every SHACMAN truck ships with parts from globally recognised suppliers:

  • Cummins ISM / ISMe / ISD — engines from 180 to 560 horsepower
  • Weichai WP series — Chinese-made alternative, factory-tuned
  • FAST transmissions — 9 / 12 / 16-speed manual or AMT
  • HANDE axles — 7.5 to 32-ton heavy-duty drive axles
  • ZF steering — German engineering on X3000 flagship
  • JOST 5th wheel — 50 mm coupling rated 16 tons

From 6×4 dump trucks for copper mines in Zambia to 4×2 distribution vans in Lagos, SHACMAN builds for the real world — heavy loads, bad roads, and operators who can't afford downtime.
