Import Notes for Uganda

What to know before importing

  • EAC Certificate of Origin (Form E) — preferential tariff
  • Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) ASYCUDA World system
  • Pre-shipment inspection by Bureau Veritas or SGS for high-value imports
Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from Uganda buyers

What's the most popular SHACMAN truck in Uganda?

X3000 6×4 tractor head — for Mombasa-Kampala-Juba long-haul. The F3000 6×4 and 8×4 rigid are close behind for construction and mining.

Where do SHACMAN trucks arrive in Uganda?

Through Mombasa (Kenya) — about 80% of Uganda's imports — then overland to Kampala. We arrange shipping and inland logistics as part of the CIF package.

Is there SHACMAN dealer support in Uganda?

Yes — SHACMAN has active dealers in Kampala with parts inventory. We coordinate with them for warranty and service support.
